APC HOA Systems

Development preview

Run your homeowners’ association billing, requests and records in one place.

APC HOA Systems handles dues, water bills, resident requests and notices, with each association’s data kept separate.

Contact us about HOA Systems

What you get

Built for homeowners’ associations, their office staff, treasurers, officers and residents.

In development. The features below run in our test environment, using demonstration data. Nothing is live yet.

Dues and water billing In testing

Bill monthly dues and metered water, with penalties added automatically when bills go unpaid.

Meter reading on a handheld In testing

Readers record meters on a handheld, working offline and syncing when connected.

Resident portal In testing

Residents see their own bills, payments, statements and requests in one place.

Requests and approvals In testing

Residents request permits, IDs and stickers online; the right person approves each request.

Counter and transfer payments In testing

Record counter payments, and confirm members’ bank transfers with a second person’s check.

Announcements and notices In testing

Post announcements to members by email and in the app, after approval.

Facilities and job orders In testing

Book facilities and track maintenance job orders from request to completion.

Management reports In testing

Collections, income, requests and turnaround reports, exportable to CSV and PDF.

Accounting feed In testing

Billing, payment and refund events are recorded for APC Simple Accounting; delivery is switched off for now.

Planned: matching payments against the bank’s own statement; backups and recovery for each association’s data; automatic detection of a suspected data breach (today staff record incidents); and switching on the accounting feed once APC Simple Accounting agrees how events are received. The feed sends billing, payment, allocation and refund events; it does not post entries directly and carries no expenses.

Trust and security

Separate space per association In testing

Each association’s data is kept apart by the database itself, so one cannot see another’s.

Sign-in protection In testing

Residents sign in with a password; approving staff and meter readers use a second code.

Roles and two-person approval In testing

Staff see and do only what their role allows; key changes need two people.

Audit trail In testing

Every record notes who created or changed it, and when.

Data incident records In testing

Incidents are logged with a 72-hour notice clock; automatic detection is not built yet.

Export when you leave In testing

When an association leaves, its data is exported to it as files.

Plainly, what is not in place: nothing is hosted for customers yet, backups and recovery are not yet running, and no certification or compliance claim is made.

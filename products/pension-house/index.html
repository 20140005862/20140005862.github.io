APC Pension House

Run your rooms, guests and bills from one simple place.

Booking, check-in, billing and reports for pension houses, lodges and small inns, with your records kept separate from every other customer’s.

What you get

Built for pension houses, lodges and small inns in the Philippines.

Be one of the first. Pension House is heading into private beta, and we’d love your pension house, lodge or inn to be among the first to use it. The features below run in our test environment.

A cloud-hosted subscription service: nothing to install on your own server. One service for many hotels, each with its own private records.

Rooms and rates

Set up your rooms and their rates once, and change them any time.

Booking calendar

See a week of room availability at a glance, and book from any free day.

No double bookings

The system refuses a booking that overlaps another on the same room.

Fast check-in

Check in a booked guest, or a walk-in, in one step, with ID recorded.

Guest folio

Add charges, payments and discounts to a guest’s bill as the stay goes on.

Check-out and statement

Check out, close the bill, and print a clear statement of account.

Company accounts

Bill a stay to a company, track what it owes, and record its payments.

Daily and yearly reports

Daily collections, receipts, room income and occupancy, and your best guests and rooms.

Tourism report export

Produce the monthly Department of Tourism DAE1-B report on demand.

Planned: a front-desk mobile app that keeps working offline, so staff can see arrivals and in-house guests, check guests in, post charges and payments, and check guests out. When the internet returns, anything that cannot be applied is shown clearly for a person to resolve.

Trust and security

Your data stays yours In testing

The service is shared by many hotels, yet each hotel’s records are kept apart at the database level, and one hotel cannot see another’s.

Sign-in protection In testing

Staff sign in with a password and, where switched on, a code from an authenticator app. Supervisors are required to use one.

Who can do what In testing

Roles such as Front Desk, Supervisor and Manager control which actions a person can take, and changes are recorded in an audit trail.

Guest ID care In testing

Guest ID numbers are masked in lists and shown in full only to staff who need them.

Backups and recovery Planned

Automated database backups with a 24-hour recovery target.

Guest data and privacy Planned

A privacy notice, retention rules, guest data deletion and a breach procedure are approved to be built. No compliance claim is made.
